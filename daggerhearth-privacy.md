# Daggerhearth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Daggerhearth is made by Beau Nouvelle in Melbourne, Australia.

Your characters belong to you. I don't run servers that store them, I don't sell data, and the app has no ads or third-party analytics.

## Your characters

Characters, notes, homebrew items and any photos you add are stored on your device.

If iCloud is turned on for the app, they also sync through your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account, and I can't read it. It's handled under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can turn syncing off in the iOS Settings app under your name, then iCloud.

Deleting a character in the app deletes it from iCloud too. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store. I never see your name, email or payment details.

The app uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) to check whether you're subscribed. RevenueCat receives:

- a random ID the app creates, which isn't linked to your name or email
- your purchases and subscription status from the App Store
- basic details such as your device's iOS version, the app version and your App Store country
- when the subscription screen is shown
- your IP address, as part of any internet connection

This is only used to unlock what you've paid for and to show me how subscriptions are going overall.

## Analytics

The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking of its own. If you've chosen to share analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage numbers.
